# Enterprise Audit Data Retention Policy

## Archived Audit Logs
Archived audit logs are retained for 365 days by default. Purging records before the retention period expires requires written approval from the designated security owner and a linked change ticket. Legal holds override the standard retention clock and prevent deletion until the hold is released by an authorized owner.

## Evidence and Provenance
Every retention operation must record the policy version, requestor identity, approval reference, time of execution, and list of affected records. The audit record should remain immutable even after the associated operational data is deleted. Documents containing personal or regulated information must follow the stricter applicable retention requirement.
